# MoodBeats "Aurora" Design System
# Unique warm-dark direction with amber/gold accents

## Design Direction: "Aurora"
A premium music discovery experience inspired by northern lights.
Warm dark tones with amber/gold accents. Frosted glass surfaces.
Distinct from typical cold-blue dark UIs.

## Color Palette
- Background: #08080f (deep space)
- Surface: #0f0f1a (elevated surface)  
- Card: rgba(18, 18, 32, 0.65) + backdrop-filter blur
- Primary: #f59e0b (warm amber)
- Primary Hover: #d97706
- Secondary: #fb7185 (coral)
- Success: #34d399 (mint)
- Error: #f87171 (soft red)
- Text Primary: #faf5ef (warm white)
- Text Secondary: #9a8f82 (warm gray)
- Text Muted: #5c554b

## Typography
- Font: Inter (400, 500, 600, 700, 800)
- Headings: -0.03em letter-spacing
- Body: -0.01em letter-spacing

## Components
- Cards: Frosted glass, warm border glow on hover
- Buttons: Amber primary, warm secondary
- Tabs: Floating pill bar
- Badges: Mood-specific warm colors
- Player: Bottom bar with amber progress
